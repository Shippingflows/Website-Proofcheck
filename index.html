The gap

The scanner checks the print. Nobody checks the file.

Print-inspection systems verify that the press reproduced the proof faithfully. But if the wrong value is already baked into the file, the print passes — and the error ships with a clean report attached. Today that last line of defense is a person, comparing documents line by line by eye. Ten reviewers will read the same die-line ten different ways, and one missed storage condition, ingredient, or kit reference becomes a reprint, a rejected lot, or a recall.

ProofCheck is the gate before print: was the file correct in the first place?

How it works

Two files in. Every difference out.

01

Upload both files

Your approved master and the supplier's proof. PDF, PNG, JPG, or TIFF — including scans of physically printed samples.

Questions

Asked by every quality team we talk to.

Does this replace our print-inspection system?+

No. Print inspection verifies the press reproduced the proof correctly — after printing. ProofCheck verifies the file was correct in the first place — before printing. They're two gates in the same quality chain, and ProofCheck runs alongside whatever inspection setup you already use.

Can it check physical samples?+

Yes — scan the printed sample and upload the scan as the proof. Flatbed or wide-format scanner output works (PDF, TIFF, JPG). Results depend on capture quality, so a clean 300–600 dpi scan of the label area works best.

Does it decide on its own?+

Never. It flags, ranks, and shows evidence. Your reviewer confirms or dismisses every finding and signs off on every review.

Who can see our documents?+

Only your organization. Files are stored privately, access is controlled, and documents are deleted on request. We're happy to sign your NDA before a pilot.

What file types and sizes?+

PDF, PNG, JPG, TIFF, and HEIC — up to 50 MB and 20 PDF pages per file today. Larger needs? Tell us in the pilot form.

What does it cost?+

Pilot first. After that, simple monthly pricing based on your review volume — no hardware, no per-station licenses, no long contract. We'll propose a number once we've seen your actual volume in the pilot.
